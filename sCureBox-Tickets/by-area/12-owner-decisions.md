## Owner decisions & standing rules (mandatory)

These rules come from the product owner. They override anything else in the ticket files. Where a ticket, the master code (sCureBox-CS 1.0.152) or an older document says something different, the rule here wins. If a rule here looks wrong or impossible, raise it as an open question. Do not quietly pick a different behaviour.

Sources:
- `SCure-DEV/docs/MIGRATION_TO_SCUREBOX_CS.md` section 7: flags F-01..F-76, the "DECIDED BY RULES" notes and section 8.4.
- The owner's standing rules from the project memory.

Each rule is written as the required behaviour of the final system, not as migration history. "As master" means "what sCureBox-CS does". That behaviour is stated concretely, and it was checked against the 1.0.152 tree where the value matters. Pure EMB code-placement notes (which EMB project or folder a type lived in) are left out unless they change behaviour.

Baseline: sCureBox-CS **1.0.152**. Master changed two values after the 1.0.140 snapshot the migration used, and the table uses the 1.0.152 value:
- The BOFA default is now 10 % (it was 30 %).
- Err 9007 is now judged on the ramp only.
