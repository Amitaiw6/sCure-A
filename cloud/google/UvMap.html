Where the measured volume sits in the chamber. The grid is centred in X and Y: the middle point is the middle of the chamber, and the outer points are ±85 mm across and ±75 mm deep. In height it starts 80 mm above the chamber floor and runs up to 380 mm, in ten 33.3 mm steps. Everything below is labelled in those chamber coordinates. The chamber itself is 350 × 240 × 400 mm, drawn as the dashed box: 90 mm is unmeasured at each side in X, 45 mm in Y, 80 mm below the lowest reading and 20 mm above the highest.
