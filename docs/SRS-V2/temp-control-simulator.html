Plant model (chamber physics)

Heating is the machine's real ON/OFF thermostat: element ON at/below target − hysteresis, OFF at the target, last state kept inside the band (no chatter). Cooling is the real PI rate loop: the chamber-fan PWM tracks the °C/min setpoint (pwm = 50 + Kp·err + Ki·∫err, 2 s samples, damper open), and stops at the target.
